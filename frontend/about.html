Our story

Woven in India, worn with pride

Keep it beautiful

Saree care guide

Silk sarees

  • • Dry clean only, and never more than twice a year.
  • • Air the saree in shade for a few hours before wearing.
  • • Wrap in soft muslin — never plastic, which traps moisture.
  • • Refold along a new line every three months to protect the zari.
  • • Keep perfume and deodorant away from the border and pallu.

Cotton & handloom

  • • Gentle hand wash in cold water with a mild detergent.
  • • Never wring — roll in a towel and press out the water.
  • • Dry in shade to protect the natural dyes.
  • • Starch lightly to keep the weave crisp.
  • • Iron on medium heat while slightly damp.
